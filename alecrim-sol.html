About the brand

Alecrim e Sol was born to support entrepreneurship and bring beauty and meaning to every special moment. For three years, we have delighted customers with a varied selection of quality products, perfect for gifting with warmth and authenticity. Made in Bahia, each item carries the culture, care and spirit of our people.

Our journey began with customers from the local market, tourists and older generations looking for something special. Now, we are ready to reach further and welcome mall shoppers who value exclusivity, convenience and products with a story to tell.

Whether you are choosing a gift or treating yourself, you will find something here to make every occasion unforgettable.

Debrief

  • 01

    Objective

    Positioning

    Build recognition for the variety and quality of the products, offering unique gifts while celebrating the talent and spirit of Bahia.

  • 02

    Audience

    Primary & secondary

    Primary: women aged 30–40 seeking exclusive, authentic, high-quality products, especially mall shoppers. Secondary: tourists in Salvador, older adults and market-goers looking for unique gift options.

    Visual direction

    Bright, sunlit colors inspired by summer, Salvador and Barra Beach, paired with minimal typography and graphic elements inspired by rosemary.

Typography

The Seasons was chosen to reflect the brand’s minimalist essence and convey authentic, joyful emotions. Its softness and clarity make reading pleasant and welcoming, connecting with the vibrant energy of Salvador and Bahia.

The secondary typeface Stolzl complements The Seasons. Its narrower, elegant forms add sophistication and modernity, creating a balanced visual hierarchy.
